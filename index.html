Why Temperature Changes Can Matter

Windshield glass expands and contracts as temperatures change. A damaged section of glass may be more vulnerable to spreading when exposed to significant temperature differences.

For example, a windshield that has been exposed to cold conditions can experience additional stress when the vehicle interior suddenly becomes much warmer. Similarly, direct heat or rapid temperature changes can affect an existing crack.

This is one reason drivers should not assume that a small chip will remain unchanged indefinitely.
